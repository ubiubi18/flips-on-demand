# Direct escrow v7 candidate

This is an **undeployed research contract**. It has no administrator, pause key, withdrawal method, or privileged settlement role. Any caller may deploy it with one pinned public seed offer ID. After that bundle is independently proved and its canonical keys are publicly disclosed in the secondary queue, any caller can activate the market by depositing exactly 10,000 iDNA. That deposit has no redeemable share and stays locked in the contract.

The next available bundle is always the oldest queued first sale. Resales follow in their own oldest-first queue. The one proved public seed stays in the resale queue as repeat-sale inventory, so completed sales, refunds, cancellation, and head expiry cannot empty the flip side after activation. It can be bought and requeued even when it is the only offer. Its keys are already disclosed and need no private delivery. Its author may also list a separate private bundle.

The buyer's price for one bundle is `min(1,000 iDNA, ceil(R / (Q + 9)))`, where `R` is the pricing reserve excluding held bonds and insurance, and `Q` is the number of currently queued bundles. Ten virtual inventory units calibrate the initial 10,000 iDNA and one real seed bundle to a 1,000 iDNA quote; the first additional private listing lowers it. Virtual units are a pricing parameter, not saleable flips. The buyer payment stays in escrow until a successful trade or refund. A first-sale author receives the price the buyer actually locked; a resale pays half to the original author and adds the other half to the reserve. Listings receive no immediate payout. Prices fall as bundles are listed and rise as queued supply is bought; they have a 1,000 iDNA ceiling. Strict queue priority still limits which bundle a buyer can choose.

Every seller posts a delivery bond. An author who has not yet proved the original flip content also posts fraud collateral equal to the 1,000 iDNA price cap. The buyer posts a proof-cost bond. After locally checking delivered keys, the buyer can confirm. Otherwise the seller has the contract's three-day fallback window to prove the original content and publicly reveal its keys. On failure, the buyer can recover the entire price and buyer bond plus the seller's delivery bond. Anyone can clear an unbought private head after seven days **as the selectable head**; a public seed rotates to the resale tail instead.
On a valid public fallback following an explicit buyer challenge, a capped part of the buyer's bond reimburses the seller even if the original content was proved before listing; any unused bond returns to the buyer. If the seller uses public fallback without a buyer challenge, the whole buyer bond returns to the buyer.

For the epoch-230 pilot, `proveBundle` also accepts an independently sealed content receipt bound to the final deployed v6 contract, but only while its author-epoch offer is qualified and has no order. The receipt still has to match the offer, the original flip inventory, the canonical session roots, and the pinned guest image. This migration proves original content and keys; it does not prove a v7 private-message delivery. An unproved funded fallback still requires a v7-bound receipt. Publicly revealing the same keys in v7 also reveals them to buyers in any other market.

The 10,000 iDNA deposit anchors the curve; it is not paid to first-sale authors. Buyer payments cover seller payouts. Without real buyers, listing more bundles lowers the displayed price but pays sellers nothing and leaves the deposit in place. This is a curve-priced queue for distinct bundles, not a fungible-token AMM with redeemable liquidity-provider shares.

The current desktop market page reads v6 snapshots and has no v7 signing, delivery, or dispute flow. Do not use it to transact with this candidate or reuse a v6 transaction: v7 `cancel` takes **zero attached value**, and an accidental payment to a nonpayable method can be stranded. WASM runtime checks exercise synthetic queue, payout, refund, and sealed content-proof paths; a real v7 proof, complete on-chain trade, real fee measurement, and independent security review remain release gates. Public flip previews from the Idena explorer are optional browsing data and do not establish content proof. Fee increases can require a seller bond top-up; the oldest offer can then wait until the seller acts or its head timeout expires.
